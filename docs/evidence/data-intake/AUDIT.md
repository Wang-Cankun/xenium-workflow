# Archive audit

Scope: the completed `trial-intake`, `trial-analysis`, `trial-scope` and `trial-conflict`. All four reports, their actual saved commands/output logs, necessary supporting JSON, minimal synthetic receiving/package inputs and source documents are preserved verbatim. Coordinator criteria are separate and explicitly marked as not supplied to candidates. Copied skills, separate profile files, caches, original source fixtures and coordinator snapshot files are excluded.

Copy verification used direct byte equality, not hashes, for every copied file. Relative links in all four original reports were checked within the archive; results are in `coordinator/archive-audit-results.json`. The manifest lists packaged files other than itself and the root `README.md`, with byte size, role and original relative source for copied artifacts. It covers trial artifacts and archive audit files at packaging, not later coordinator additions such as execution summaries, deployment logs or test logs.

Input snapshot comparison: intake 9/9 receiving files, analysis 5/5 package files, scope 8/8 delivery files and conflict 2/2 receiving files match exactly, with no missing or added paths. Archived inputs equal their current originals. The snapshots do not cover intake's three top-level source documents, scope's agreement, or conflict's request/agreement/source-observation documents; these were checked only for exact copying. No snapshot embeds its capture time. Coordinator context says scope and conflict snapshots predate their trials. The analysis snapshot was taken after its trial started, so earlier modifications cannot be excluded. Intake chronology was not independently established by this audit. These results do not establish sender-side byte identity or whole-trial immutability for analysis. Raw snapshots are excluded; comparison outcomes and snapshot filenames are retained.

Static script review found no standalone portable replay: intake locates its new root but lacks its inventory helper; analysis shells and join checker hardcode the original workspace and its metadata/full scans lack their helper. Analysis inventory/runtime also expects a profile and macOS stat. Scope's targeted checker resolves archived inputs, but exclusive output creation conflicts with its existing saved output. Conflict's targeted checker resolves paths from cwd and would overwrite its saved output. Scope and conflict full workflows lack both helpers. Runtime and missing dependencies are detailed in trial READMEs; helper transitive dependencies were not audited. Original reports and replay claims have not been edited; archive notes explain relocation limits. No archived scripts were executed.

Only synthetic fixtures were copied. No canonical repository, deployment, real research data or external service was touched; no checksum computation, installation, sending or repair was performed. This audit packages observed evidence and does not independently score criteria or establish general workflow reliability. The coordinator-reported scope and conflict passes are recorded separately in `coordinator/evaluation-notes.md`.
